# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ChromaVault aims to be a respectful space for people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information or secrets.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that do not fit the project's scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainer privately through a contact method listed on the repository profile, if available. Avoid posting sensitive personal or security information publicly.
